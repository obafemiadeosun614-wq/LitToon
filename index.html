Redemption Road

by Elma Shaw · Chapter 1

Chapter 1 in plain English

  1. Ten years after the civil war, Bendu Lewis and her family hold a remembrance for her grandmother, Granny May.
  2. Granny May's body was never found. So the family buries a coffin holding her belongings, like her Bible and photos, as a symbol.
  3. Bendu remembers the war. She pushed her sick grandmother in a wheelbarrow while crowds ran for safety. People told her to leave Granny May behind, but she refused.
  4. Granny May died on the road. Bendu was crying beside her and did not notice about thirty rebel soldiers coming.
  5. The soldiers treated her roughly. Their leader, Commander Cobra, stopped one soldier from hitting her, then questioned her and ordered her to join them.
  6. Back in the present, friends and family comfort Bendu. She is thankful, but she is hurting because she cannot tell them everything.

Words to know

Remembrance: a gathering to honour someone who has died.
Symbolic: standing for something else. The coffin stands for Granny May because her body was never found.
Flashback: when the story jumps back to an earlier time. Chapter 1 does this with the war memory.
Hint to notice: Bendu feels she cannot tell everyone everything. The author is hinting at a secret.
